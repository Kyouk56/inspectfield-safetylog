1. Overview & Local-First Philosophy

InspectField SafetyLog ("the Application") is an offline-capable, local-first commercial life safety compliance utility built for fire safety inspectors, facility managers, and safety compliance officers.

We believe that your facility's safety records belong exclusively to you. By default, all inspection logs, equipment registries, technician digital signatures, defect tags, and audit histories are stored directly on your local device using an encrypted SQLite Room database and hardware-isolated Android Keystore memory. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or inspection data.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Application collects and stores the following records strictly within local device storage:

  • Equipment Asset Metadata: Tag IDs, serial numbers, equipment types (ABC Dry Chemical, CO2, Class K, AED, Eye Wash), physical location descriptions, and periodic inspection frequency protocols (NFPA 10, NFPA 25, NFPA 72, OSHA 1910.151).
  • Inspection Checklists & Records: Passed/Deficient verification responses, pressure gauge readings, tamper seal observations, defect descriptions, and timestamps.
  • Digital Technician Signatures: Touch-drawn digital inspector signatures collected on-device and bound into certified compliance reports.
  • Inspector Profile Metadata: Inspector name, company affiliation, certification license numbers (e.g., NICET, State Fire Marshal), and contact information for certified PDF compliance certificates.

3. Android Device Permissions & Data Usage

To deliver commercial inspection workflows and maintain compliance schedules, the Application requests the following Android permissions:

A. Camera (android.permission.CAMERA)

Used exclusively when you choose to take a photo of an equipment asset or document a physical defect during an inspection. Photos are stored locally on your device or in designated application directories. The camera is never accessed in the background, and photos are never uploaded to advertising networks.

B. Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Used solely to display high-importance scheduled inspection due-date reminders that you configure. The Application does not send marketing, promotional, or remote push tracking notifications.

C. Exact Alarms (android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Used to trigger calendar-precise reminder alarms for recurring NFPA and OSHA inspection schedules (monthly 30-day visual checks, quarterly, semi-annual, annual). The alarm manager schedules are calculated and stored strictly on-device.

D. Device Reboot (android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)

Used solely to restore your scheduled inspection alarms in the system AlarmManager after your device restarts so compliance due dates are not missed.

E. Device Calendar (Zero-Permission Intent)

When you tap "Add to Device Calendar", the Application launches your device's native Calendar app via Android's standard ACTION_INSERT contract with pre-populated event details and recurrence rules. The Application does not read your calendar or request calendar permissions.

4. Cryptographic Security & Hardware Keystore

  • Android Keystore MasterKey: Authentication tokens, session hashes, and sensitive secrets are encrypted using AES-256-GCM cipher keys backed by hardware Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) or StrongBox Hardware Security Modules (HSM) where available on your device.
  • Crash Log Vault: Application crash logs and diagnostic traces are encrypted and stored in a private local vault (CrashLogVault). Cloud telemetry dispatch is strictly opt-in and disabled by default. You can inspect or clear crash traces at any time in Settings.

5. Third-Party Services & Sub-Processors

A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Free-Tier Users: The standard free tier includes non-intrusive banner advertisements served via the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Ad-Free & Paid Subscribers: Upgrading to the Ad-Free Pass ($2.99), Individual Pro ($0.99/mo), or Enterprise ($0.50/seat/mo) completely and permanently disables all AdMob banner instances and network requests.

B. Google Play In-App Billing

Subscription and in-app purchase transactions are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. InspectField SafetyLog does not collect, receive, or store your credit card or financial account numbers. All financial transactions are subject to the Google Play Terms of Service.

C. Optional Cloud Drive & Webhook Backups

If you configure Company OAuth, Team Sync, or Google Drive folder exports, data transmission occurs exclusively over HTTPS/TLS to the endpoints or folders that you or your organization explicitly authorize.

6. Account and Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Hex Engine Labs and InspectField SafetyLog provide complete user autonomy over personal data and account records in full compliance with Google Play Store data safety requirements.

A. How to Request Account & Data Deletion

  • In-App Immediate Purge: Open Settings > Storage & Privacy Vault and select "Clear All Local Data & Reset Identity Vault". This permanently purges all local databases, cached credentials, and inspector profiles from your device's hardware-isolated storage. To revoke OAuth session keys, tap "Sign Out & Revoke Credentials" under Profile.
  • External / Web Deletion Request (Without App Installed): If you do not have the application installed, you can submit an account and data deletion request directly by emailing our compliance team at hexenginelabs@gmail.com with the subject "InspectField Account Deletion Request". Specify the Google account email or company domain associated with your login. Requests are confirmed within 48 hours and processed within 7 business days.

B. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: Profile credentials, inspector name, contact details, certification license numbers, cached OAuth session tokens, equipment registries, inspection logs, defect photos, and digital signatures.
  • Retained Data & Retention Period: Because InspectField SafetyLog operates on a local-first architecture, no inspection records or credentials are retained on our servers once deleted (0-day retention). In-app purchase and subscription transactions are maintained strictly by Google Play In-App Billing pursuant to statutory requirements.

C. Data Portability

Prior to deletion, you may export your complete historical compliance audits and equipment logs in PDF or CSV format directly from the Reports tab.

7. Children's Privacy

InspectField SafetyLog is designed for commercial facility safety managers, technicians, and compliance officers (ages 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
