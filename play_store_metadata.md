## 2. Full Description (4,000 characters max)

```text
SafetyLog is a standalone, local-first commercial compliance utility built specifically for fire safety technicians, facility managers, and safety compliance officers. Streamline routine periodic inspections for fire extinguishers, emergency eye-wash stations, AED units, and life-safety equipment without requiring internet connectivity or cloud accounts.

KEY FEATURES:

✔ LOCAL-FIRST & OFFLINE-READY
Perform inspections in basements, mechanical rooms, remote job sites, and industrial warehouses without a cellular or Wi-Fi connection. All records and signatures remain securely stored on your device.

✔ NFPA 10 COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST
Standard 5-point life safety verification:
1. Pressure gauge indicator in the operable green zone
2. Hose, horn, and discharge nozzle free of cracks or blockages
3. Safety pull pin seated with tamper seal intact
4. Legible operating instructions and hazard rating signage
5. 36-inch clear unobstructed physical access

✔ DIGITAL TECHNICIAN SIGNATURES
Collect and bind verified digital touch signatures directly on-site with an intuitive built-in signature pad.

✔ SIGNED AUDIT PDF EXPORTS
Generate professional US Letter compliance reports on-device using Android's native PDF engine. Includes facility metadata, technician credentials (NICET / State Cert #), timestamps, color-coded status badges (PASSED / DEFICIENT), defect notes, and embedded signature blocks ready for immediate email or cloud dispatch.

✔ ASSET MANAGEMENT & AUDIT LOGS
Easily register commercial fire extinguishers (ABC Dry Chemical, CO2, Class K, Clean Agent), AED stations, and safety kits with Tag IDs, location descriptions, and serial numbers. View historical inspection logs per asset.

✔ FREEMIUM SIMPLICITY
Inspect up to 5 safety assets free every calendar month, or upgrade to SafetyLog Pro for unlimited inspections and commercial reporting.
```
